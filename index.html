Professional profile

Security engineering at the boundary of hardware identity, runtime trust, and resilient infrastructure.

My work focuses on a hard question behind modern infrastructure: how do you trust and identify a system before allowing it into a critical environment? That includes secure identity, attestation, trusted measurements, and evidence that the system is running what the business expects it to run.

I bring experience across motor vehicle manufacturing and enterprise technology environments, including Ford Motor Company and Oracle, with a focus on practical security architecture for systems that must remain trustworthy, recoverable, and scalable.
