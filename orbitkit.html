OrbitKit

A simple personal toolkit for compound interest, mortgage planning, and fasting tracking. OrbitKit is local-first, does not require an account, and is designed for quick everyday planning.

View on App Store Get support

Compound interest

Estimate long-term growth and compare contribution versus interest with a clear visual breakdown.

Mortgage calculator

Calculate monthly payment estimates and review amortization visuals for planning home financing.

Fasting tracker

Track elapsed fasting time and see milestone achievements without setting up an account.

Local-first

Calculator inputs and fasting data stay on your device. OrbitKit does not require login or analytics tracking.
